# The push after a session

What `wb` does with the issue's branch and the change once the commits of a session are taken back. The session holds no login to the code host.

| ID | Requirement | Ver | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| WB-PUSH-1 | After the commits of a session of an issue are taken back and nothing was refused, `wb start` shall push the issue's branch to `origin` by `git push origin <commit>:refs/heads/<branch>`, as the person and outside the sandbox, where `watchbill.yml` has `change_of_branch` and `draft_change`. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-2 | `wb start` shall push only the stamp's branch, at the last commit taken back, and never with force. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-3 | `wb start` shall say on standard error what it will push, the commit and the branch, and the command, before it pushes. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-4 | Where a commit was refused at the taking back, or work was left uncommitted, `wb start` shall push nothing, say why, and say the commands of WB-VIEW-14 and WB-VIEW-15. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-5 | A push that git refuses shall end `wb start` with 1, with what git said, and with no change opened. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-6 | After a build whose branch was pushed, where `change_of_branch` prints no change, `wb start` shall open one by `draft_change`, with the description on standard input, after it says the command and the description; where it prints one, or more than one, it shall open none. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-7 | After the tester and the fix, `wb start` shall push and run neither `change_of_branch` nor `draft_change`. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-8 | `{description}` shall be filled with `/run/watchbill/description`, a file of a new folder that `wb start` binds writable at `/run/watchbill` in the sandbox, outside the view's tree, and removes after it has read the file. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-9 | Where the description file is missing or holds only white space, the change shall be opened with a description that names the issue and says that the session wrote none. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-10 | Without `change_of_branch` or `draft_change` in `watchbill.yml`, or with `--no-push`, `wb start` shall push nothing, say why, say the commands of WB-VIEW-14 and WB-VIEW-15, and run none of them. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-11 | After a session of the tester, the build or the fix of an issue, the session's folder shall hold a file `push`: `pushed: <commit>` or `not pushed: <why>`, and after a build that was pushed `change: <number>` or `change: not opened: <why>`. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-12 | No login of the code host shall be inside the sandbox of a session: the variables of the logins of `gh`, `glab` and ssh, the secrets GitLab CI hands a job, and the address of the session bus are not handed in, and a runner's bind that holds or lies in a login of the code host (below the home, below `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, a keyring below `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, the folder of the ssh agent's socket), or that is or holds `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, is refused with 1 before anything is posted. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-13 | Each notice `wb` posts to a tracker (a stamp made, a session's findings, a handover, questions, answers, done) shall name the hash of the commit on the job's ref that it copies. Issue: #240. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-14 | `wb` shall read back none of the notices it posts to a tracker. Issue: #240. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-15 | Where the session did not end with 0, `wb start` shall push nothing and say why. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-16 | `wb start` shall push only after the tester, the build and the fix of an issue; after any other session it shall say the commands of WB-VIEW-14 and WB-VIEW-15, and run none of them. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-17 | A description file that is not a plain file, is over 64 KiB, is not UTF-8, holds a control character but a line break or a tab, a line separator or a bidirectional control, or holds `-` alone once white space is trimmed, shall open no change, and `wb start` shall end with 1, saying why. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-18 | `--description` given to `wb start` without `--prompt`, `--stamp` or `--parts` shall be refused as used wrongly, with 2. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-19 | `wb init` shall write a `draft_change` that reads the description from standard input and pushes nothing: `--body-file -` for GitHub, `--push=false` and `--description "$(cat)"` for GitLab. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-20 | `wb start <role> <issue> --take-back` shall push nothing, and say the commands of WB-VIEW-14 and WB-VIEW-15; `--no-push` with `--take-back` shall be used wrongly, with 2. Issue: #151. | T | Implemented |
| WB-PUSH-21 | Before a session of the tester, the build, the review or the fix of an issue, and before the stamp is posted, `wb start` shall run `issue_text` and `issue_comment_bodies` as the person, outside the sandbox, each through the cap of 1 MiB, and bring in, read-only at `/run/watchbill/in` in the sandbox and outside the view's tree, `issue.md`, what `issue_text` printed, whole, and `questions`, the comments whose first line is a question or an answer of that role (`[questions] <issue>.<role>`, `[answers] <issue>.<role>`, or a form of revision 0.0.8), whole and in order, and no other; a pattern missing or failing, or more than 1 MiB, shall refuse the start as a limit, with 1, before anything is posted. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-22 | For the review and the fix, `wb start` shall also run `change_text` and `change_comment_bodies` and bring in `change.md`, what `change_text` printed, whole, and `change.comments`, the comments of the change whose first line is `[review] <on> round` or `[fix] <on> round`, where `<on>` is the issue, or the change where the issue is none; where the issue is none, `questions` is read from the change's comments, and no `issue.md` is brought in. A pattern missing or failing, or more than 1 MiB, shall refuse the start as WB-PUSH-21 does. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-23 | For the review and the fix, `wb start` shall bring in `change.diff`, the diff of the branch against its merge base with the default branch, limited to the paths of `changes` and the role's `reads`, or whole where there is no issue's file, through the cap of 1 MiB; a diff that cannot be had shall refuse the start as a limit before anything is posted. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-24 | `wb init` shall write `change_text`, which prints the change's description alone: `gh pr view {change} --repo <repository> --json body --jq .body` for GitHub, `glab mr view {change} --repo <address> -F json \| jq -r .description` for GitLab, and none for another server; without it, the review and the fix shall be refused as a limit before anything is posted, naming the line and that `wb upgrade` adds it. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-25 | After a session of the tester, the build, the review or the fix, `wb start` shall read `/run/watchbill/questions`, each question starting at a line `q<n>: <what is unclear>`, and post those not asked yet in one comment `[questions] <on>.<role> q1 q2 …`, each question's first line as its line and the rest under `<details>`, by `issue_comment`, or by `change_comment` where the issue is none, after saying it; a file whose text does not start with a question is refused. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-26 | After a session of the review or the fix, `wb start` shall post `/run/watchbill/change_comment` on the change by `change_comment`, after saying it, under a first line of its own, `[review] <on> round <n>` or `[fix] <on> round <n>`; only where the session ended with 0, and for the fix only where its branch was pushed; otherwise it shall say why it is not posted, and the text. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-27 | After a session of any role, `wb start` shall post `/run/watchbill/needs_person`, unless it holds only white space or `none`, on the issue by `issue_comment`, or on the change where the issue is none, after saying it, under a first line of its own, `[needs_person] <on>.<role>` (`[needs_person] <issue>` for the planner), whatever the session ended with. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-28 | A file a session writes for `wb` to post (WB-PUSH-25 to 27) that is not a plain file, is over 64 KiB, is not UTF-8, or holds a control character but a line break or a tab, a line separator or a bidirectional control, shall be refused: nothing of it is posted or shown, and `wb start` ends with 1, saying why as a limit. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-29 | `{brought}`, `{questions}`, `{change_comment}` and `{needs_person}` shall be filled with `/run/watchbill/in`, `/run/watchbill/questions`, `/run/watchbill/change_comment` and `/run/watchbill/needs_person`; the folder `wb` makes for them is removed after the session. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
| WB-PUSH-30 | `--brought`, `--questions`, `--change_comment` and `--needs_person` given to `wb start` without `--prompt`, `--stamp` or `--parts` shall be refused as used wrongly, with 2. Issue: #153. | T | Draft |
